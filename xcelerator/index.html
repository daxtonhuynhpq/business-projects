XCELERATORPITCH COMPETITION

In 2022, Siemens introduced Xcelerator — a next-generation digital platform set to redefine industrial digital transformation. As part of a regional initiative to build go-to-market strategy, ASEAN launched a competition challenging teams to craft and deliver the most impactful 2-minute pitch.

Organization
Project typeGo-to-market storytelling
RegionVietnam → ASEAN Finals

01 / The starting point

Making a complex digital platform memorable.

While Xcelerator was a strategic priority for Siemens, internal employees and partners had limited recall of its overarching value proposition.

02 / Audience insight

The communication challenge

A targeted survey of partner employees (covering both technical and non-technical roles) revealed:

Platform value recall63%

63% recalled only specific functions, not the platform’s strategic value.

Non-technical audience≈30%

Despite playing a critical role in shaping go-to-market strategies, non-technical staff (≈30% of respondents) struggled to retain information from lengthy technical brochures — the traditional marketing tools.

03 / Creative execution

Strategic actions

01

Commercial storytelling × technical education

Led a creative direction blending commercial storytelling with technical product education, leveraging music as the core delivery medium to simplify and humanize key product values.

02

Creative campaign production

Rewrote the lyrics of a commercial song to embed product features and benefits, producing a music video set during a marathon — visually symbolizing digital acceleration and personal growth.

03

Human-centered communication

Designed the format to create an emotional connection with both technical and non-technical audiences.

04 / Competition outcomes

Achievements

National round WINNER

Won the Vietnam national round and represented Siemens Vietnam at the ASEAN Finals in Singapore.

ASEAN Finals TOP 2

Placed Top 2 across ASEAN.
