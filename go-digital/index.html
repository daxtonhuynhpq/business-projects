GO DIGITAL!B2B DISTRIBUTORS

Until recently, few believed Siemens’ industrial products could be sold via e-commerce. Most distributors’ websites in Vietnam were static information hubs. The B2B Distributor Digital Business initiative aimed to turn them into fully functional webshops, starting with product data alignment and digital commerce integration.
