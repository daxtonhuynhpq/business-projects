STREAMLINE END-TO-END OPERATIONS WITH SELF-BUILT ZERO-COST SYSTEM

AMI faced the challenge of scaling operations with extremely limited resources. With no budget for expensive ERP, CRM, or accounting systems, and no headcount for specialized staff, the company sought a zero-cost, Excel-based solution to automate workflows across Sales, Academic, HR, and Finance.

Challenges

Limited resources

As an early-stage startup, AMI could not afford to hire specialized staff. Some employees had to wear various hats, making workload reduction essential.

No budget for professional systems

ERP, CRM, and class management tools in Vietnam are prohibitively expensive for a small education business.

Fragmented data flows

Manual duplication of data across teams (Sales, Academic, HR, Finance) not only consumed time and effort but also created room for errors. The lack of integrated data flows meant that a single update had to be repeated in multiple places instead of cascading automatically into outputs such as class assignments, receivables, payroll, and P&L reporting.
