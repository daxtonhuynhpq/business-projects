#GO2025 Initiative ZERO-COST STRATEGY

Founded in March 2024, AMI Education set out with a bold mission: to bridge the gap in access to high-quality English education in Bac Lieu, a rural province in Vietnam. Launched in June 2024, the #GO2025 Initiative aimed to reach monthly break-even by June 2025. Built around the “Road to University” vision, it targeted high school students with A1–B2 English levels and was anchored on three interconnected pillars: Sales, Marketing, and Academic Delivery.

Community-driven constraints

“Score-driven” culture

Vietnamese students often take extra English classes with their own school teachers, focusing on boosting school GPA.

This deep-rooted culture made it difficult to shift student mindsets toward developing “real” English competencies.

Price sensitivity

The average monthly income in Bac Lieu was ~CAD 310; families spent ~CAD 100 per child on education (Bac Lieu People’s Committee, 2024), while AMI’s average tuition was ~CAD 285 to ensure expense coverage.

Burdened IELTS teacher costs

With ~95% of applicants lacking IELTS teaching qualifications, the shortage of competent teachers in Bac Lieu drove hiring costs to 40% of total monthly expenses.

#GO2025 Initiative

The strategy connected customer access, student experience and commercial sustainability instead of treating Sales, Marketing and Academic Delivery as separate workstreams.

Marketing

  • Reached 2,400+ students via career orientation and scholarship events (Road to University, Coffee Talk, Orientation Talks), and in partnership with four largest high schools in the province.
  • Co-organized free Mega Test series with the province’s biggest gifted high school, offering value upfront and capturing qualified leads.

Sales

  • Built zero-cost, revenue-sharing partnerships through a success-fee model with 10 private education institutions and 10+ local vendors (cafés, bookstores, etc.).
  • Secured an MoU with Bac Lieu High School for the Gifted, offering free 20-hour IELTS hackathons and automatic discounts for medalists of the National Excellent Student Selection Contest.

Academic delivery

  • Pioneered a hybrid learning model that enabled access to top-tier teachers from anywhere in the world - optimizing cost per student while ensuring consistently high academic outcomes.
  • Launched the 1-2-3-6 Framework - a structured feedback process to monitor learning experience and student progress at four key milestones: 1 day after start, 2-week trial, 3-month midpoint, and 6-month course end.
  • Launched “Growth Talk” - a no-cost, 1-on-1 mentorship program, offering students direct access to AMI’s leadership team for tailored career orientation sessions. This initiative reinforced AMI’s identity as a lifelong education mentor - not just a language trainer.
