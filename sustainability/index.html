SUSTAINABILITY@SIEMENS VIETNAM

In 2023, Siemens Digital Industries Vietnam set an ambitious goal: to become the first country in ASEAN to accelerate sustainability initiatives internally and across its partner ecosystem – with the aim of turning Sustainability from a mere concept into a driver of sales growth.

01 / The starting point

Turning sustainability into a business growth driver.

The initiative focused on understanding internal awareness, partner readiness, regulatory pressures and untapped customer opportunities before translating sustainability into relevant market offerings.

03 / From insight to market action

Strategic actions

01

Strategic reframing

Localized Siemens’ global sustainability priorities to the Vietnam market context by conducting research on customer pain points, government policies and international market trends. The goal was to address the key question: Where could Siemens’ solutions deliver tangible value to its customers in Vietnam?

Delivered 10 internal training sessions over 5 months (incl. ASEAN leadership and 36 key partners) to position sustainability as a lever for business growth through energy efficiency, cost reduction, and operational optimization.

02

Industry-specific sales kits

Collaborated with ASEAN and HQ teams to develop industry-specific “Sustainability” sales kits to tailor offerings to various industries, mapping out regulatory and operational pain points.

Go-to-market

Rolled out Sales Kits to prioritize key accounts within the target pool of 2,000 companies.
